Fried Rice

Home

Description

Fried rice is a dish made from cooked rice stir-fried in a wok or frying pan with oil, aromatics, and mixed ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups of cold rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 2-3 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 2-3 tablespoon neutral oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the ingredients
  2. Cook and scramble the eggs
  3. Cook the Aromatics
  4. Fry the rice
  5. Season and sauce
  6. Combine and plate
